Common questions

Everything you need to know
about commercial bridging.

What types of commercial property can be bridged?+
Offices, retail, industrial, warehouses, mixed-use, semi-commercial, leisure and hospitality, healthcare, and more. We also have lenders for unusual or complex assets.
What LTV is available on commercial bridging?+
Up to 70–75% LTV on most commercial assets. Lower LTV is typically available on well-tenanted, standard commercial property.
Can you bridge a property that's been declined elsewhere?+
Yes — this is our speciality. Listed buildings, vacant properties, short leases, complex title, planning issues — we have specialist lenders for exactly these situations.
How is a commercial bridging loan assessed?+
Lenders assess the asset value and type, LTV, exit strategy, borrower experience and credit profile. For commercial property, the tenancy situation and covenant strength are also key factors.
