Common questions

Everything you need
to know.

Is there a cost to register?+
Commercial terms, including any fees and commission, will be confirmed in writing by our team before you submit cases.
How quickly can I start submitting cases?+
Broker registration is a demonstration at this stage. In the live service we will explain how accounts are set up.
Which brokers can submit cases?+
We will confirm broker eligibility and any regulatory requirements before accepting cases. Contact our team if you are unsure.
What types of cases can I submit?+
Residential and commercial bridging, development finance, auction finance, refurbishment loans, mezzanine finance, and second charge bridging. We also welcome foreign national and complex structure cases.
How is commission calculated and paid?+
Commission arrangements are agreed with each broker and confirmed in writing. Please contact our team to discuss.
Can I submit cases for clients who have adverse credit?+
Yes. Some specialist lenders serve borrowers with adverse credit histories, including CCJs, defaults and missed payments. Our team will identify suitable lenders for each borrower profile.
