Common questions

Everything you need
to know.

Is it really free to register?+
Yes — completely free. No registration fee, no monthly subscription, no minimum case volume requirement. You only benefit when cases complete and commission is paid.
How quickly can I start submitting cases?+
Immediately after registration. Once your account is verified (typically within 2 hours), your portal is fully active and you can submit your first case straight away.
Do you accept cases from all FCA-registered brokers?+
Yes — we work with all FCA-registered credit brokers with bridging and specialist finance permissions. If you're unsure whether your permissions cover bridging, our team can advise.
What types of cases can I submit?+
Residential and commercial bridging, development finance, auction finance, refurbishment loans, mezzanine finance, and second charge bridging. We also welcome foreign national and complex structure cases that other platforms decline.
How is commission calculated and paid?+
Commission is calculated as a percentage of the loan amount (see our commission structure above). It is paid within 5 working days of completion confirmation. All commission is tracked in real time in your portal dashboard.
Can I submit cases for clients who have adverse credit?+
Yes. We have lenders on our panel who specifically serve borrowers with adverse credit histories including CCJs, defaults and missed payments. Our matching engine identifies the most appropriate lenders for each borrower profile.
