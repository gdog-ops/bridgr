Illustrative estimates only — not a lender offer, quote or DIP

Enter an exact loan amount and term to see an illustrative estimate of the cost. The rate and fees are assumptions you can change, not lender rates or offers. Speak to our team for terms that fit your case.

About this estimate
How the estimate works.
Interest
Monthly: loan amount × monthly rate × number of months. Rolled up: the loan compounds at the monthly rate for the term, and the interest is repaid on exit. Retained: shown using the same figure as rolled up in this estimate. The rate is an assumption you can change, not a lender rate.
Fees
Arrangement and exit fees are a percentage of the loan. Valuation and legal fees are fixed amounts. All four are assumptions you can change. Real fees depend on the lender, the property and the case.
Loan to value
The loan amount divided by the property value you enter. It uses your own figures and is not a valuation. Any maximum loan to value is set by the lender, the asset and the borrower.
What this is not
The estimate is illustrative. It is not a lender offer, a quote or a Decision in Principle, and it does not mean finance is available. To talk it through, call an underwriter or start an enquiry.
