UK market context
How bridging costs work.
Interest rates
UK bridging rates currently range from 0.52% to 2.00% per month. The average in 2025 is around 0.76% per month. Your rate depends on LTV, asset type, exit strategy and borrower profile. Lower LTV = lower rate.
Interest structures
You can pay interest monthly (like a mortgage), roll it up to exit (most common for investors — no monthly payments), or retain it upfront from the loan. Rolled-up interest means a higher loan balance at exit.
Fees to expect
Arrangement fee: typically 1–2%. Exit fee: 0–1% (many lenders charge none). Valuation: £600–£5,000+ depending on asset. Legal fees: £1,500–£4,000+ for both parties. Broker fee: varies.
Complex deals
Listed buildings, mixed-use, distressed assets, second charges and high LTV deals typically attract higher rates. MCP specialises in structuring these — deals that mainstream lenders won't touch.
